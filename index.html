About the company

27 years in sheet metal, now under one roof.

SPAK Metaal Craft is run by Kailash Punamchand Jagdale, who has spent 27 years in sheet metal fabrication with deep, hands-on knowledge of laser cutting, CNC bending, and fabrication.

He started as an operator in 1999 and worked his way through production, supervision, and manufacturing management roles before running his own contract manufacturing business for six years. Two years in sales and marketing with a Belgium-based machine manufacturer rounded out the commercial side of the trade.

In September 2025, that experience became SPAK Metaal Craft — a manufacturing unit built from the ground up with advanced laser cutting, bending, and welding equipment.
